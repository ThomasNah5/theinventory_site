We didn't start The Inventory because the market was ready.

We started it because we believed something: that the next generation of transformative technology wouldn't come **from** Africa — it would be built **with** Africa. That distinction matters more than it sounds.

## The problem with imported solutions

Most digital products deployed across the continent were designed elsewhere — for different infrastructures, different behaviors, different economic realities. They arrive polished and often fail silently. Not because the people are unready, but because the tools were built without context.

Network reliability, payment infrastructure, language, trust — these aren't edge cases here. They're the center.

## What we're doing about it

We're a small team. On purpose. Smaller means we move carefully, say no to the wrong things, and stay close to the problems we're actually solving.

Every product we build starts with a question: **would this work if the power went out, the network dropped to 2G, and the user had never seen a tutorial?**

If yes, we're on the right track.

## What's next

We're building in the open — publishing our thinking, our mistakes, and our progress. This blog is part of that.

Follow along.
